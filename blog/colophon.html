The colophon

The boring, useful part: what this is, what it runs on, and what it refuses to do.

Why "Offcuts"

When a printer trims a sheet to size, the strips left over are offcuts. They're not the job, but they're still paper, and some of them are too good to throw away. That's roughly what this is: the bits trimmed off the portfolio, the parts that didn't fit on a spec sheet.

How it's made

Hand-written HTML and CSS. No framework, no build step, no CMS, no package manager. The type is Fraunces for reading, Space Mono for labels, and Caveat for the pencil marks. All three are self-hosted, subset to Latin.

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages from the same repository as the portfolio. The RSS feed is a static XML file I update by hand, which is either charming or a bug, depending on how many sheets there are.

What it doesn't do

  • No analytics. I don't know you're here, and I'm not going to ask a script to find out.
  • No comments. There's an email address at the bottom; it works better.
  • No newsletter. If you want new sheets, use RSS.
  • No cookies, apart from the one that remembers whether you turned the lights off. It stays in your browser.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, tell me. I'll fix it and, where it matters, say that I did. I'd rather have a corrected sheet than a clean-looking one.
