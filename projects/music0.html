About this one

Music0 is the online evolution of Musico — rebuilt in v1.0.0 from a Tauri shell into a unified Compose Multiplatform player backed by a pure-Rust JNI core (musico_core). Zero WebView overhead: one Material 3 Expressive codebase across Android, Linux, macOS and Windows.

Streams resolve cleanly through a tiered pipeline — smart LRU cache, JioSaavn direct 320 kbps AAC, InnerTube / Piped fast paths, and yt-dlp fallback. It ships with Lyrics Studio (synced LRCLIB karaoke + Cinema mode), a 5-band Equalizer with Bass Boost & AutoEQ, Radio moods, Spotify/Apple Music playlist import, and a VirusTotal 0/66 signed Android APK.

What's in it

  • Compose Multiplatform UI + pure-Rust musico_core JNI audio & catalog engine
  • Tiered streaming via InnerTube, JioSaavn & yt-dlp with smart LRU cache
  • Lyrics Studio with synced LRCLIB karaoke & Cinema mode, plus Radio moods & stations
  • 5-band Equalizer (Bass Boost & AutoEQ) and Spotify / Apple Music / CSV / M3U playlist import
  • Signed Android APK (VirusTotal 0/66) & checksummed desktop packages for Linux, macOS and Windows
