About this one

Music0 is the online evolution of Musico: search YouTube via InnerTube, play via yt-dlp, and carry your library across Linux, Windows, macOS and Android. It exists because desktop music players kept getting worse — heavier, uglier, less yours.

Shipped as a real product: six releases in quick succession, each with signed installers for .deb, .rpm, AppImage, .exe, .msi, .dmg and an Android APK. If there's a platform you use, there's a build for it.

What's in it

  • InnerTube search + yt-dlp playback — streams without a second app
  • One codebase, four platforms — Linux, Windows, macOS, Android
  • Signed release artifacts with SHA256SUMS for verification
  • The flagship download of this portfolio — start here
