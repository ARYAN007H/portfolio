About this one

Every Google account comes with 15GB free. Have three accounts? That's 45GB scattered across three logins. Cloudy unifies it — one app, one view, all your storage.

Written in Rust and shipped cross-platform from v0.1.0 with AppImage, .deb, .rpm, Windows and macOS installers. Your free storage, finally in one place.

What's in it

  • Unify multiple Google accounts' free storage in one app
  • Rust-native, cross-platform from the first release
  • AppImage, .deb, .rpm, .exe, .msi, .dmg coverage
  • Live site at aryan007h.github.io/cloudy
