About this one

CamDroid turns the phone already in your pocket into a studio-grade webcam. An Android app (Kotlin + Compose) streams to a Linux companion (Rust + FFmpeg) that plugs into v4l2loopback — so it shows up as a real camera in every app.

Near-zero latency (~30ms), up to 4K at 60 FPS, AAC audio. Studio stage controls and protocol hardening landed in v2.1.0. Grab the APK for your phone and the .deb/.rpm for your desktop.

What's in it

  • ~30ms latency · up to 4K @ 60 FPS · AAC microphone
  • v4l2loopback virtual camera — works in OBS, Meet, Zoom, everything
  • Studio stage controls & hardened streaming protocol
  • Android APK + Linux .deb / .rpm in one release
