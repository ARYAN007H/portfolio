About this one

No chess.js. No engine libraries. Every rule of chess — castling, en passant, pins, the fifty-move rule — plus three generations of alpha-beta search, written from zero and verified move by move.

It runs in a Web Worker at ~1M+ nps, with a self-play tuning loop and a trained NNUE in the pipeline. Play it live in the browser — no install, no download, just the engine.

What's in it

  • From-scratch rules, move generation, evaluation & search
  • ~1M+ nodes/sec in a Web Worker — doesn't block the UI
  • Perft-verified correctness, with a test suite
  • Self-play tuning loop + NNUE training pipeline
