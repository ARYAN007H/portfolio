About this one

Musico is where the Music0 story begins: a music player for Linux, written in Rust, because the existing options felt like they were designed by people who don't listen to music on their computers.

The local-first player evolved into the online Music0, but the original still ships — v0.4.0-rc2 with AppImage, .deb, .rpm, Windows and macOS builds, plus a live site.

What's in it

  • Local library player, Linux-first, Rust-native
  • The ancestor of Music0 — same design DNA, offline edition
  • Full cross-platform releases with SHA256SUMS
  • Live site at aryan007h.github.io/Musico
