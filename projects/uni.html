About this one

U&I is a private app for exactly two people: no ads, no AI training, one-click export, encrypted media, cancel-safe billing. Built first for long-distance couples — 'built for when you can't hug them' — because they need it most.

Flutter + Supabase, Android-first, iOS second, web as demo. After a full rebuild in Oct 2026 it's getting a new design system, local-first data layer and a private R2 media pipeline. Live demo at humon.vercel.app.

What's in it

  • Exactly-two-people: Home, Chat, Us (memory timeline), Plans (countdowns)
  • No ads, no AI training, encrypted media, one-click export
  • Flutter + Supabase, local-first data layer
  • Live demo at humon.vercel.app
