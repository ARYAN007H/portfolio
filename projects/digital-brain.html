About this one

Digital Brain is an attempt at a second memory: it fetches data from various conversations and processes it locally to build connections between them and improve context over time.

Local-first by design — your conversations stay on your machine while the brain learns the links between them. An experiment in what personal AI should have been: private, yours, and cumulative.

What's in it

  • Ingests data from multiple conversation sources
  • Local processing — connections built on your machine
  • Improves context across conversations over time
  • An experiment in private, cumulative personal AI
